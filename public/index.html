Questions

Straight answers.

Can I cheat Disconnect or bypass the block?

It depends on the lock level you chose, which is the point. Soft lock you can open instantly — it only shows you a reminder of why you blocked the app in the first place. Medium lock makes you wait five minutes, and cancelling restarts the timer rather than skipping it. Hard lock has no override and no backdoor: the only way back in is your buddy approving the request.

Do I need a buddy to use Disconnect?

No. Soft lock and Medium lock work entirely on their own, with no buddy and no pairing. For a lot of people a gentle reminder or a five-minute cooldown is enough on its own. A buddy is only required for Hard lock, which is the level where someone else holds the key.

What can my buddy actually see?

Very little, by design. When you ask to unlock something, your buddy sees the name of the rule you are trying to open and any optional reason you choose to write. That is all.

They do not see which apps you have blocked, your schedule, your screen time statistics, or your usage history. The list of apps you block never leaves your device at all.

What happens if my buddy doesn't respond?

Nothing unlocks. An unlock request stays pending until your buddy actively approves or denies it — there is no timeout that quietly lets you back in. If they are asleep or busy, the block simply holds, which is the behaviour Hard lock exists to guarantee.

How is this different from Apple's built-in Screen Time?

Screen Time limits are enforced by a passcode you hold yourself, so in a weak moment you are asking yourself for permission — and you always say yes. Disconnect's Hard lock moves that decision to another person, so getting back in takes someone else's deliberate approval rather than your own tap.

Soft and Medium locks work more like Screen Time, with a reminder or a cooldown, for the times you don't need the stronger version.

Does my buddy need the app? Does being a buddy change their phone?

Your buddy does need Disconnect installed to receive and answer requests, and being a buddy is free. It changes nothing about their own phone, their own apps, or their own screen time — they simply get a notification and approve or deny it with one tap.

When my buddy approves, am I unlocked forever?

No. An approval opens the app for a set window, and when that window expires the block goes back on automatically. Your buddy can also deny a request outright, and add a short reason when they do.

Is Disconnect free?

Yes, and the free version is genuinely usable rather than a trial. It includes one blocking rule, one buddy, and all three lock levels — so Hard lock, the buddy accountability that makes Disconnect different, works without paying anything.

Disconnect Pro lifts the limits on rules and buddies. It is available as a monthly or yearly subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Being someone else's buddy is always free.

What do I get in the free version versus Pro?

Free gives you one rule, one buddy, and every lock level including Hard lock. That is enough to block your single worst app and hand the key to a friend, which is the core of how Disconnect works.

Pro is for people who want several rules running at once — a work schedule, a sleep schedule, a weekend rule — or more than one buddy. Nothing about the blocking itself gets stronger with Pro; you just get more of it.

Is there an Android version?

Not currently. Disconnect is an iPhone app and requires iOS 16 or later. Because both people need the app, you and your buddy both need to be on iPhone.

What data does Disconnect collect?

The apps you block and the schedule you set are stored only on your device and never sent to a server. Disconnect does not collect Screen Time statistics, usage data, or your location.

What is stored is what the accountability system needs to work: your name and email from Sign in with Apple, who you are paired with, the unlock requests passing between you, and a push token to deliver notifications. Data is processed by Firebase, with RevenueCat handling subscriptions and Apple handling payments. Nothing is sold or used for advertising.

How long does setup take?

Under two minutes. Pick the apps you want blocked, choose a lock level and a schedule, and it runs quietly in the background from there. There is no dashboard to check and no streak to maintain.

How do I delete my account and my data?

In the app, under Settings → Delete account. That permanently removes your user record, your buddy relationships and your unlock request history within 30 days. You can also email support@bytehood.com to access, port, or restrict your data.
